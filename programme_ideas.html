Programme Innovation Workshop — Ideas Overview

Date: June 2026 Format: Pre-work and in-person workshop Focus: AI-related study programmes

This document summarises the programme hypotheses generated ahead of and during the innovation workshop held in June 2026. The goal of the workshop was to identify opportunities for new two-year study programmes at NQF level 5, with a particular focus on how AI is reshaping industries and professional roles.

Participants were asked to develop concrete programme hypotheses covering the problem the programme solves, target students, graduate roles, potential industry partners, and subject areas. The aim was to narrow the field to three programmes worth developing further after summer.
