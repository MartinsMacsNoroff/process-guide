Study Plan Development Noroff Vocational College
Your shared workspace for developing new study programs at Noroff Vocational College — from the first strategic idea to NOKUT accreditation. Read more ↓
Each program follows an 8-step process across three phases: Strategy & Planning, Program Development, and Accreditation. At every step you will find clear guidance, the right people, and AI tools that generate key documents automatically — saving time and ensuring consistency across all programs.

Create a new program to get started, or open an existing one to continue where you left off. Your manager can follow progress for all programs directly from this dashboard.
How to use this tool
1
Create a new program — click the button below to start. Give it a name.
2
Work through the 8 steps — each step guides you with activities, people involved, and AI tools to generate documents.
3
Generate documents with AI — Step 1 makes a program summary, Step 2 a mandate, Step 3 a project plan. Each builds on the previous.
4
Documents are saved per program — everything survives tab switches. Come back any time and continue where you left off.
5
Track progress here — this dashboard shows all programs, their phase, completion and status at a glance.
